## 🧙‍♂️ SECRETS, PRO-TIPS & MINI-GUIDES

### ✨ THE PIXAI MAGIC TRICK (Metadata Injection)
PixAI strips all metadata from its downloaded images. PGP has a secret trick to fix this:
1. Open your downloaded PixAI image in PGP's Gallery.
2. Go to the PixAI website and simply **COPY** the prompt text or the whole page (`Ctrl`+`C`).
3. Go back to PGP and press `Ctrl` + `V`.
4. **MAGIC!** The app will instantly parse your clipboard, inject the hidden metadata into your image, and save a new, fully restored PNG file automatically.

### ⚡ WORKFLOW EXAMPLE (The Gelbooru Method)
Tired of manually cleaning copied tags? Try this:
1. Copy a massive block of text directly from a Gelbooru image page.
2. Paste it in the Input box.
3. Click **"Gelbooru"** tool ➔ Click **"Sort"** ➔ Click **"Professional"** ➔ Click **"Copy"**.
> *You just turned garbage into a masterpiece prompt in 3 seconds!*

### 💾 STORAGE SAVING TIP

If you want to free up hard drive space, **you can safely delete these folders!** The app WILL NOT break. It will automatically detect they are missing and seamlessly switch to using cloud-based voices (Edge TTS) instead.
